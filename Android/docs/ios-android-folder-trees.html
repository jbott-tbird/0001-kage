Where the folders diverge

Email address

iOS uses Core/Sources/EmailAddress/. Android keeps EmailAddress.kt alongside the account models.

Managers and setup

iOS groups managers with models and autoconfiguration in Core. Android places their work in app repositories, a ViewModel, and data/setup/.

JMAP

iOS has a dedicated JMAP/ package. This Android tree has no JMAP module.

Database storage

Android has a separate Room data/local/ folder. This iOS checkout has no implemented counterpart to that entity layer.
